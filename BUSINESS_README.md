# Hire AI - Business Overview

## Executive Summary
Hire AI (Smart Hire) is an intelligent, automated talent acquisition platform designed to streamline the recruitment process. It eliminates the manual effort of sifting through crowded email inboxes, downloading resumes, and entering data into Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS). By leveraging advanced Artificial Intelligence (LLMs), Hire AI automatically reads emails, extracts applicant information, and organizes it into a centralized, searchable dashboard.

## Core Value Proposition
- **Time Savings**: Automates hours of manual data entry by extracting candidate profiles directly from email attachments.
- **Centralized Candidate Pool**: Aggregates applicants from multiple sources (Gmail, Outlook, custom domains) into one unified interface.
- **Intelligent Screening**: Uses AI to parse unstructured resumes (PDFs, Word documents) and categorize candidates based on skills, experience, and education.
- **Automated Communication**: Engages candidates instantly with automated acknowledgement emails and follow-ups for missing information.

## Key Features

### 1. Automated Inbox Monitoring
Hire AI connects directly to your organization's recruitment email addresses. It continuously monitors the inbox for incoming emails that match specific keywords (e.g., "resume", "job", "application").

### 2. AI-Powered Resume Parsing
When a resume is detected, the AI engine takes over. Instead of simple keyword matching, it "reads" the resume contextually. It extracts critical data points such as:
- Full Name, Email, and Phone Number
- Total Years of Experience
- Core Skills and Technologies
- Educational Background
- Current Location and Expected Salary (if available)

### 3. Job Description (JD) Matching
Recruiters can create specific Job profiles within the system. The platform can semantically match incoming candidates against these Job Descriptions, assigning a match score to help recruiters prioritize the most relevant applicants instantly.

### 4. Smart Auto-Replies
The system can be configured to automatically reply to candidates:
- **Acknowledgement**: Letting the candidate know their application was received.
- **Missing Data Follow-up**: If the AI notices the resume is missing a phone number or current location, it can automatically email the candidate back requesting that specific missing information.

### 5. Admin & Analytics Dashboard
A comprehensive dashboard provides real-time insights into recruitment metrics:
- Candidate volume over time
- Source of candidates (which email accounts are performing best)
- Easy-to-use interface to approve, reject, or export candidate data to Excel/CSV.

## Workflow Summary
1. **Connect**: Admin links Outlook or Gmail accounts via the integrations dashboard.
2. **Listen**: System silently polls the connected inboxes 24/7.
3. **Extract**: Resumes are identified, downloaded, and sent to the AI for parsing.
4. **Store**: Structured candidate profiles are created in the database.
5. **Action**: Recruiters log into the dashboard to review, match, and contact top talent.
